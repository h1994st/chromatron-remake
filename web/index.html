How to play

Goal

Light up every target with a beam of its colour. Drag pieces from the toolbox onto the board to bend, split and filter the lasers. Each level’s hint text introduces new pieces. Solving a level unlocks more, and the numbers in the level strip change colour as you go.Solving a level unlocks more. The level picker shows which levels are open and solved.

Mouse

  • Drag a piece between the toolbox and the board.
  • Click a piece to turn it anticlockwise.
  • Right-click (or Shift, Option or Control and click) to turn it clockwise.
  • Click a number in the level strip to go to that level.
  • Click a level in the picker (or Levels) to go to it.
  • Scroll over the game to change level (switch it off with the toolbar button).

Touch

  • Drag to move a piece. The piece floats above your finger and lands on the square it hovers over.
  • Tap a piece to turn it anticlockwise. A small wobble still counts as a tap.
  • On a touch screen, switch on Turn clockwise (the round arrow in the toolbar) and taps turn clockwise.
  • Hold the phone upright for a bigger board, and pick a level with Levels in the toolbar, or tap Next level after a win. Turn it sideways to see the whole original screen.
  • Pick a level with Levels, or tap Next ▶ after a win. Upright or sideways, the board fills the screen.

Keys

  • Space = next level
  • - previous level
  • R reset the level
  • ? this help

Solutions

  • ⌘C and ⌘V, or the Actions menu, copy a level’s solution as text and paste one back.
  • The text works in the Mac apps too.

Progress

Saved in this browser. Each game reopens on your last level.
